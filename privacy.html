Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

K-Storyteller Library is a free website for Korean learners. "kstoryteller" is a personal tool used only by the channel owner to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

Students and visitors

This website does not ask you to sign in and does not collect your personal information. Videos and thumbnails load from YouTube, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Google user data

  • What it accesses: only the channel owner's own YouTube data, such as video titles, descriptions, playlists and captions, through YouTube API Services.
  • How it is used: to keep the list of videos on this website up to date and to manage the owner's channels.
  • Where it is stored: login tokens and working files stay only on the owner's computer. Public video titles, links and sentences appear on this website.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold or given to anyone.
  • Removing access: access can be removed at any time in Google Account permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
